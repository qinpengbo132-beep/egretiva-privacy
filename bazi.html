八字 Bazi 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-08-01 · Effective date: 2026-08-01

中文版

应用名称：八字 Bazi（下称“本应用”）
开发者：Egretiva

1. 我们收集的数据：无

本应用不收集任何个人信息。没有账户体系，没有注册登录，没有统计、分析或广告 SDK，不采集设备标识符，不进行任何形式的数据上报。

2. 本地存储

  • 您输入的出生日期、时间、地点等排盘信息仅在您的设备本地计算与保存（应用沙箱），不会离开您的设备。
  • 您配置的 AI 服务 API Key 仅保存在系统安全存储中（iOS Keychain / Android Keystore），不以明文落盘，不写入日志。
  • 删除应用或清除档案即彻底删除上述全部数据，无需也无法向我们请求删除。

3. 第三方 AI 服务（用户自选、直连）

  • 仅当您主动发起 AI 解读时，本应用会将当次排盘数据直接发送至您自行选择并配置的 AI 服务商（如 OpenAI、Anthropic 或您指定的兼容端点）。
  • 该请求由您的设备直连该服务商，不经过我们的任何服务器；我们不中转、不查看、不存储请求与响应内容。
  • 在 GDPR 口径下，对该次请求的数据处理者为您自选的服务商，本应用非 controller 亦非 processor。请参阅您所选服务商的隐私政策：
    • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
    • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • 我们不出售、不共享您的个人信息（CCPA/CPRA：无 "Do Not Sell" 适用场景，因为根本不发生收集）。

4. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童信息（本应用不收集任何人的信息）。

5. 政策变更

如本政策发生变更，新版本将随应用更新发布于本页面，并更新生效日期。由于我们不收集您的联系方式，无法逐一通知，请定期查阅。

6. 联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请联系：support@egretiva.com

English Version

App: 八字 Bazi ("the App")
Developer: Egretiva

1. Data We Collect: None

The App collects no personal data. There is no account system, no sign-up, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no device identifiers, and no telemetry of any kind.

2. On-Device Storage

  • Birth date, time, and place you enter are computed and stored only on your device (the app sandbox) and never leave it.
  • API keys you configure for AI services are stored solely in the system secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), never in plaintext files or logs.
  • Deleting the App or clearing your profiles permanently erases all of the above. There is nothing to request from us because we hold nothing.

3. Third-Party AI Services (User-Selected, Direct Connection)

  • Only when you explicitly request an AI reading does the App send that single chart's data directly to the AI provider you chose and configured (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, or a compatible endpoint you specify).
  • The request goes straight from your device to that provider. We do not proxy, inspect, or store requests or responses, and operate no servers in this path.
  • Under GDPR, the data controller/processor for that request is the provider you selected, not the App. Please refer to your provider's privacy policy:
    • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
    • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • We do not sell or share personal information (CCPA/CPRA: there is no "sale" scenario because nothing is collected).

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children (it collects information from no one).

5. Changes to This Policy

Any changes will be published at this page with an updated effective date. As we hold no contact information for you, please check back periodically.

6. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact: support@egretiva.com
