中文

本地计算与存储

四柱及五行等排盘计算在设备上完成。命盘档案、会话、分析缓存、书签和阅读位置保存在应用本地。应用没有自建账号、会员、广告或分析上报服务，开发者不运营接收命盘与对话内容的业务服务器。系统备份或设备迁移由操作系统和您的设置管理。

外部AI

仅在您主动发起AI功能时，应用直连您选择并配置的服务商，默认支持DeepSeek。发送内容可能包括相关出生资料、命盘计算结果、姓名（如提供）、所问问题、相关典籍段落以及用于继续对话的历史上下文。自由输入的个人内容也会进入该请求。应用不通过开发者AI中转服务器；对话及结果会在您的设备本地保存。

您的API Key用于向所选服务商认证，并保存在iOS Keychain，不放入普通命盘/会话文件。第三方对请求的保存和使用取决于其条款、您的账户设置和适用服务，不保证请求不留存或不用于其任何处理。可查阅DeepSeek隐私政策：https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html 。其他服务商或自定义端点适用各自政策。

分享与导出

主动分享会将选定内容交给您选择的系统分享目标。典籍电子文本导出包含内置原文、可用译稿、来源和许可，不含您的命盘、会话或笔记。已导出的副本由您及接收目标管理。

删除与停用

在应用中删除档案或会话会移除对应本地记录。卸载应用通常移除沙箱数据，但不保证删除Keychain项目、系统备份、服务商记录或已分享的副本。可在设置中替换/删除Key，并在服务商处撤销Key或管理其保存的数据。

联系与更新

隐私问题请联系support@egretiva.com。本政策随实际功能与数据处理变化更新。商店隐私问卷应按实际发送、留存及服务商处理核对，不以“本地排盘”代替完整披露。

English

Local calculation and storage

Chart calculation runs on your device. Profiles, conversations, analysis cache, bookmarks and reading positions are stored locally. The app has no developer-operated account, membership, advertising or analytics service. The developer runs no business server receiving chart or conversation content. Operating-system backup and migration depend on your device settings.

External AI

Only when you request AI does the app connect directly to your selected provider, defaulting to DeepSeek. Requests may include relevant birth details, calculated chart facts, names if supplied, your question, cited text and prior conversation context. Personal content entered in free text is included in that request. There is no developer AI proxy; conversations and responses are stored locally on your device.

Your API key authenticates the request and is held in iOS Keychain, not ordinary profile or conversation files. Provider retention and processing depend on its terms, your account settings and the service used. No universal non-retention or non-training promise is made. DeepSeek policy: https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html . Other providers or custom endpoints have their own policies.

Sharing and export

Sharing sends selected content to the destination you choose through the operating system. Book-text exports contain bundled originals, available drafts, sources and licenses, not your profiles, conversations or notes. You and the receiving destination control exported copies.

Deletion and key management

Deleting a profile or conversation removes the corresponding local record. Uninstalling generally removes sandbox data, but does not guarantee removal of Keychain items, system backups, provider records or shared copies. Replace/delete keys in Settings and revoke them or manage retained data at the provider.

Contact and updates

Contact support@egretiva.com for privacy questions. This policy should reflect actual functionality and processing. App Store privacy answers require checking actual transmission and retention, including applicable provider practices, rather than assuming that local chart calculation means no data is transmitted.
