中文版

应用名称：八字 Bazi（下称“本应用”）
开发者：Egretiva

1. 我们收集的数据：无

本应用不收集任何个人信息。没有账户体系，没有注册登录，没有统计、分析或广告 SDK，不采集设备标识符，不进行任何形式的数据上报。

2. 本地存储

  • 排盘计算在您的设备上进行。案例、对话和分析缓存在应用本地保存；您主动使用 AI 或分享功能时，相关内容会发送到您选择的服务商或分享目标。
  • 您配置的 AI 服务 API Key 保存在 iOS Keychain 中；发送 AI 请求时用于向您选择的服务商认证，不写入普通案例或对话文件。
  • 删除案例或对话会移除对应的应用本地记录。卸载应用不保证清除 Keychain 项目、系统备份或您已分享的副本；如需停用 API Key，请在服务商处撤销。开发者不持有您的案例或对话副本。

3. 第三方 AI 服务（用户自选、直连）

  • 仅当您主动使用 AI 功能时，本应用会将完成该请求所需的排盘信息、相关对话和提示内容直接发送至您自行选择并配置的 AI 服务商（如 OpenAI、Anthropic 或您指定的兼容端点）。
  • 该请求由您的设备直连该服务商，不经过我们的任何服务器；我们不中转、不查看、不存储请求与响应内容。
  • 服务商对请求内容的保存和使用受其隐私政策及您的账户设置约束。请参阅您所选服务商的隐私政策：
    • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
    • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • 我们不出售、不共享您的个人信息（CCPA/CPRA：无 "Do Not Sell" 适用场景，因为根本不发生收集）。

4. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童信息（本应用不收集任何人的信息）。

5. 政策变更

如本政策发生变更，新版本将随应用更新发布于本页面，并更新生效日期。由于我们不收集您的联系方式，无法逐一通知，请定期查阅。

6. 联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请联系：support@egretiva.com

English Version

App: 八字 Bazi ("the App")
Developer: Egretiva

1. Data We Collect: None

The App collects no personal data. There is no account system, no sign-up, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no device identifiers, and no telemetry of any kind.

2. On-Device Storage

  • Chart calculations run on your device. Cases, conversations, and analysis caches are stored locally in the App. When you choose an AI or sharing feature, relevant content is sent to your selected provider or sharing destination.
  • AI API keys are stored in iOS Keychain and used to authenticate requests to your selected provider. They are not stored in ordinary case or conversation files.
  • Deleting a case or conversation removes that local App record. Uninstalling the App does not guarantee removal of Keychain items, system backups, or copies you have shared. To disable an API key, revoke it with its provider. The developer holds no copy of your cases or conversations.

3. Third-Party AI Services (User-Selected, Direct Connection)

  • Only when you choose an AI feature does the App send the chart details, relevant conversation, and instructions needed for that request directly to the AI provider you chose and configured (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, or a compatible endpoint you specify).
  • The request goes straight from your device to that provider. We do not proxy, inspect, or store requests or responses, and operate no servers in this path.
  • The provider's retention and use of request content are governed by its privacy policy and your account settings. Please refer to your provider's privacy policy:
    • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
    • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • We do not sell or share personal information (CCPA/CPRA: there is no "sale" scenario because nothing is collected).

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children (it collects information from no one).

5. Changes to This Policy

Any changes will be published at this page with an updated effective date. As we hold no contact information for you, please check back periodically.

6. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact: support@egretiva.com
