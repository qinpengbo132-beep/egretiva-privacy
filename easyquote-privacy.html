轻松报价 · Privacy Policy

更新 / Updated: 2026-10-07

本机资料 / Local data

公司、客户、银行信息、设备、图片和报价文件保存在你的 Mac 上。轻松报价不建立自有账号，不提供云端托管，不收集广告标识或使用遥测分析，也不包含 AI 调用。业务资料不会发送给开发者。

Your business records, equipment, images and documents stay on your Mac. Easy Quote has no developer-managed account, hosted workspace, advertising, analytics or built-in AI. Business records are not sent to the developer.

Apple 订阅 / Apple subscriptions

购买、恢复及订阅状态核对由 Apple StoreKit 处理。应用读取 Apple 验证的产品与交易状态以提供对应功能，不接收银行卡号或支付密码。Apple 按其隐私政策处理商店及支付信息。

Apple StoreKit handles purchases, restoration and subscription status. The app reads verified product and transaction information to provide access. It does not receive your payment card or payment password.

可选汇率 / Optional exchange rates

启用刷新时，应用向 ECB 和备用服务 Frankfurter 请求公开汇率；不附带业务记录。这些服务可能处理正常网络连接信息，例如 IP 地址。可关闭自动刷新并使用手动汇率。

Optional rate refresh requests public rates from ECB and Frankfurter without your business records. Providers may process ordinary connection data, such as an IP address. You can disable automatic refresh and set rates manually.

导出与备份 / Exports and backups

你选择文件、备份及 Agent 快照的保存位置，并自行决定是否分享。归档保留资料；删除本机内容不会删除外部文件副本。备份可用于迁移到其他设备。应用没有开发者端的业务资料副本。

You choose where exports, backups and optional Agent snapshots go and whether to share them. Archiving retains records. Removing local data does not remove external copies. Backups support device migration.
