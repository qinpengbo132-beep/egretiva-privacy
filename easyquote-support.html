轻松报价 · Support

在 Mac 上整理设备、公司资料和贸易条件，制作报价单、形式发票（PI）和独立商业发票（CI），预览实际 PDF 后再导出。

Prepare quotations, proforma invoices and separate commercial invoices on Mac, with equipment and company profiles, trade terms and actual PDF previews.

购买与恢复 / Purchases

在“设置 → 订阅”或文件导出时打开购买页，选择“恢复购买”，并使用购买时的 Apple 账号。Pro 可选自动续订的月付、年付，或一次购买的非消耗型买断；三者解锁相同的 PDF 和逐页图片导出。编辑、预览、归档和备份免费。适用本地价格以 Apple 购买页为准。

月付和年付可在应用购买页选择“管理订阅”，前往 Apple 账号订阅页面管理或取消。买断无需续费，但购买买断不会自动取消已有订阅。订阅取消后，当前权益可用至到期；到期且没有买断时仍能编辑、预览和备份。

Open Settings → Subscription, or the purchase screen when exporting, then choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account used to buy. Monthly, annual and one-time lifetime Pro all unlock PDF and page-image exports. Editing, previews, archiving and backups are free. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple’s subscription settings. Buying lifetime Pro does not automatically cancel an existing subscription.

资料迁移 / Migration

在“设置 → 通用”导出完整备份。仅迁移设备、公司和客户预设时，可导出资料库 ZIP；在兼容安装中导入并核对。资料库也支持加密离线二维码传输，以及同一局域网内配对、核对确认码后发送。离线传输需要接收完整资料码和独立解密码，确认导入预览后才写入资料。

Export a full backup in Settings → General, or use a library ZIP for presets. Compatible installations can import these files. Library transfer also supports encrypted offline QR codes and confirmed pairing on a local network. With offline QR transfer, receive the data and separate unlock code, then review the import before committing.

语言与使用范围 / Languages and scope

界面支持简体中文、英语、俄语、法语、日语和西班牙语。单据栏目语言独立支持中文、英语、西班牙语和法语；用户填写的内容不会自动翻译。公开汇率为参考值，可手动设置。Mac 不提供内置 AI、云端托管账号或自动同步。

The interface supports Simplified Chinese, English, Russian, French, Japanese and Spanish. Document labels are separately available in Chinese, English, Spanish and French; user content is not automatically translated. Exchange rates are reference information and can be entered manually. The Mac app has no built-in AI, hosted user account or automatic cloud sync.

iPhone 版本仍在开发和验证，尚未在 App Store 发布。此页面不提供 iPhone 商店下载入口。Mac 商店可用状态以实际 App Store 页面为准。

The iPhone version is still in development and validation and has not launched on the App Store. No iPhone store download is offered here. Check the actual App Store listing for Mac availability.
