轻松报价
Easy Quote for Mac

把设备资料和贸易条件，整理成可核对的商务单据。

Turn equipment details and trade terms into business documents you can review.

从报价到发票 / From quotation to invoice

创建报价单和形式发票，从 PI 生成独立商业发票。设备数量、单价、选配、多币种价格与贸易条件统一整理，查看实际 PDF 后导出。原 PI 与单据修订保留，方便追溯。

Prepare quotations and proforma invoices, then create a separate commercial invoice from a PI. Organize quantities, prices, options, currencies and trade terms; inspect the actual PDF before export. Keep the source PI and document revisions.

资料跟随工作 / Keep your work portable

公司抬头、银行账户和印章成套管理。设备预设、技术参数、图片与公司展示页支持自动分页。本机保存，ZIP 备份和迁移；也可使用加密离线二维码或经确认的局域网配对分享资料库。

Keep company headings, bank accounts and seals together. Equipment presets, technical specifications, images and company pages support automatic pagination. Store work locally, back up with ZIP and share library data through encrypted offline QR codes or confirmed local-network pairing.

先编辑，按需导出 / Create freely, export when needed

编辑、PDF 预览、归档和备份免费。Pro 月付、年付和一次买断提供相同的 PDF 与逐页图片导出权益。月付与年付自动续订；买断无需续费。适用价格在 Apple 购买页显示。

Editing, PDF previews, archiving and backups are free. Monthly, annual and lifetime Pro provide the same PDF and page-image exports. Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically; the lifetime purchase does not. Apple shows the applicable price before purchase.

界面与单据语言 / Interface and document languages

六种界面语言：简体中文、英语、俄语、法语、日语、西班牙语。单据栏目独立支持中文、英语、西班牙语和法语；不会自动翻译业务内容。

Six interface languages: Simplified Chinese, English, Russian, French, Japanese and Spanish. Document labels separately support Chinese, English, Spanish and French. Business content is not automatically translated.

Mac 版本正在准备首次 App Store 发行，商店下载入口将在实际公开可用后提供。iPhone 版本尚未在 App Store 发布。

The Mac app is preparing for its first App Store release. A store download link will be provided when it is publicly available. The iPhone version has not launched on the App Store.
